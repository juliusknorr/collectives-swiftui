# Buddha bowl

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 30 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] Roasted sweet potato
- [ ] Quinoa
- [ ] Chickpeas
- [ ] Avocado
- [ ] Pickled red cabbage
- [ ] Tahini dressing

## Steps

1. Roast sweet potato and chickpeas at 220 °C for 25 minutes.
2. Cook the quinoa.
3. Arrange everything in bowls and drizzle with dressing.

> **Tip:** A template more than a recipe. Use what you have.
