# Hollandaise

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 15 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 3 egg yolks
- [ ] 1 tbsp lemon juice
- [ ] 180 g clarified butter, warm
- [ ] Salt, cayenne

## Steps

1. Whisk yolks and lemon over a water bath until thick.
2. Drizzle in butter, whisking constantly.
3. Season and keep warm, not hot.

> **Tip:** If it splits, whisk a fresh yolk and slowly add the broken sauce.
