# Minestrone

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 50 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks
- [ ] 2 zucchini
- [ ] 400 g chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 400 g cannellini beans
- [ ] 1.5 l stock
- [ ] 100 g small pasta
- [ ] Parmesan rind

## Steps

1. Dice the vegetables and sweat them in olive oil.
2. Add tomatoes, stock and the parmesan rind, simmer 20 minutes.
3. Add beans and pasta, cook until the pasta is done.
4. Remove the rind and season.

> **Tip:** Better on day two.
