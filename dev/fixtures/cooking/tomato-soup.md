# Tomato soup

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 35 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 kg ripe tomatoes
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves
- [ ] 500 ml vegetable stock
- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] Basil
- [ ] Splash of cream

## Steps

1. Roast halved tomatoes, onion and garlic at 200 °C for 25 minutes.
2. Blend with the stock.
3. Simmer 5 minutes, season, finish with cream and basil.

> **Tip:** Roasting gives a deeper flavor than simmering raw tomatoes.
