# Fluffy pancakes

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 20 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 200 g flour
- [ ] 2 tsp baking powder
- [ ] 1 tbsp sugar
- [ ] Pinch of salt
- [ ] 250 ml milk
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 2 tbsp melted butter

## Steps

1. Whisk the dry ingredients.
2. Whisk milk, egg and butter, then fold into the flour. Small lumps are fine.
3. Rest 5 minutes.
4. Cook ladlefuls in a buttered pan until bubbles form, flip, cook 1 more minute.

> **Tip:** Rest the batter. The baking powder needs a moment and the pancakes rise higher.
