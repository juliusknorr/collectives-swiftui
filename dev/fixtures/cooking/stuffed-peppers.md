# Stuffed peppers

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 1 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 4 peppers
- [ ] 150 g rice or quinoa, cooked
- [ ] 400 g black beans
- [ ] 200 g corn
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin
- [ ] Salsa
- [ ] Grated cheese

## Steps

1. Halve and seed the peppers.
2. Mix the filling and season.
3. Fill, top with cheese, bake at 190 °C for 35 minutes.

> **Tip:** Freezes well.
