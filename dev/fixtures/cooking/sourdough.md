# Sourdough loaf

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 1 loaf | 24 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 100 g active starter
- [ ] 375 g water
- [ ] 500 g bread flour
- [ ] 10 g salt

## Steps

1. Mix starter, water and flour. Rest 1 hour.
2. Add salt, then four sets of stretch and folds over 2 hours.
3. Bulk ferment until risen by half.
4. Shape, proof overnight in the fridge.
5. Bake in a preheated Dutch oven at 250 °C, 20 minutes covered and 25 uncovered.
