# Gazpacho

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 15 min + chilling |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 kg tomatoes
- [ ] 1 cucumber
- [ ] 1 green pepper
- [ ] 1 garlic clove
- [ ] 50 g stale bread
- [ ] 80 ml olive oil
- [ ] 2 tbsp sherry vinegar

## Steps

1. Roughly chop everything.
2. Blend until very smooth, adding oil in a stream.
3. Season and chill for 2 hours.

> **Tip:** Served cold. No stove needed. ☀️
