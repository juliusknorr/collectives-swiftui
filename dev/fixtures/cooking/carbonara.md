# Spaghetti carbonara

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 20 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 200 g spaghetti
- [ ] 100 g guanciale
- [ ] 2 egg yolks + 1 egg
- [ ] 50 g pecorino
- [ ] Black pepper

## Steps

1. Crisp the guanciale in a dry pan.
2. Whisk eggs and pecorino with lots of pepper.
3. Toss drained pasta with guanciale off the heat.
4. Add the egg mix and a splash of pasta water, toss until glossy.

> **Tip:** No cream. The heat of the pasta cooks the eggs, so keep the pan off the flame.
