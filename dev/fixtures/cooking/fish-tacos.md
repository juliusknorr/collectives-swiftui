# Fish tacos

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 30 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 500 g white fish
- [ ] 1 tsp chili powder, 1 tsp cumin
- [ ] 8 corn tortillas
- [ ] Red cabbage slaw
- [ ] Lime crema
- [ ] Pickled onions

## Steps

1. Season and pan-fry the fish, flake it.
2. Warm the tortillas.
3. Fill with fish, slaw, crema and pickled onions.
