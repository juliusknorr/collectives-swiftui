# Mango sticky rice

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 1 h + soaking |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 200 g glutinous rice, soaked 4 hours
- [ ] 400 ml coconut milk
- [ ] 60 g sugar
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt
- [ ] 2 ripe mangoes
- [ ] Toasted sesame seeds

## Steps

1. Steam the rice for 25 minutes.
2. Warm most of the coconut milk with sugar and salt, pour over the rice and rest 20 minutes.
3. Thicken the remaining coconut milk for drizzling.
4. Serve with sliced mango and sesame.

> **Tip:** Ripe mango matters more than anything else here.
