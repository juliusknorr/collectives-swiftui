# Cacio e pepe

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 15 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 200 g tonnarelli or spaghetti
- [ ] 80 g pecorino, finely grated
- [ ] 2 tsp black pepper, freshly cracked

## Steps

1. Toast the pepper in a dry pan.
2. Cook pasta in less water than usual to keep it starchy.
3. Mix pecorino with a little cool pasta water to a paste.
4. Toss pasta with pepper, then the paste, adding water until creamy.

> **Tip:** Three ingredients, one technique.
