# Tahini sauce

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 1 bowl | 5 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 100 g tahini
- [ ] 1 lemon
- [ ] 1 garlic clove, grated
- [ ] Ice water

## Steps

1. Mix tahini, lemon and garlic. It will seize.
2. Whisk in ice water spoon by spoon until smooth and pourable.

> **Tip:** Goes with falafel and Buddha bowls.
