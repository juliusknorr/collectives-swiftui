# Pad thai

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 25 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 150 g flat rice noodles
- [ ] 200 g prawns or tofu
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 3 tbsp tamarind paste
- [ ] 2 tbsp fish sauce
- [ ] 2 tbsp palm sugar
- [ ] Bean sprouts, spring onion, peanuts, lime

## Steps

1. Soak noodles in warm water for 20 minutes.
2. Mix tamarind, fish sauce and sugar.
3. Stir-fry prawns, push aside, scramble the eggs.
4. Add noodles and sauce, toss 2 minutes.
5. Add sprouts and spring onion, top with peanuts and lime.

> **Tip:** Not technically pasta, but nobody complained.
