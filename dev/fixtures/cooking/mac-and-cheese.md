# Mac & cheese

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 35 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 300 g macaroni
- [ ] 40 g butter
- [ ] 40 g flour
- [ ] 500 ml milk
- [ ] 200 g mature cheddar
- [ ] 1 tsp mustard
- [ ] Breadcrumbs

## Steps

1. Cook the macaroni 2 minutes short.
2. Make a roux, whisk in milk, simmer until thick.
3. Melt in cheese and mustard.
4. Combine, top with breadcrumbs and bake at 200 °C for 15 minutes.

> **Tip:** The ampersand in the title tests URL and WebDAV encoding.
