# 100% rye bread

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 1 loaf | 1 day |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 500 g rye flour
- [ ] 100 g rye starter
- [ ] 450 ml warm water
- [ ] 12 g salt
- [ ] 1 tbsp caraway seeds

## Steps

1. Mix everything to a paste. No kneading.
2. Pour into a tin, smooth with wet hands.
3. Proof until cracks appear, 3 to 5 hours.
4. Bake at 230 °C for 15 minutes, then 200 °C for 45 minutes.
5. Wait a full day before slicing.
