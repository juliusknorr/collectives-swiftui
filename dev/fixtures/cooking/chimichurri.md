# Chimichurri

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 1 jar | 10 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] Bunch of parsley
- [ ] 2 tbsp oregano
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] 1 red chili
- [ ] 120 ml olive oil
- [ ] 3 tbsp red wine vinegar

## Steps

1. Chop everything finely by hand.
2. Mix with oil and vinegar, season.
3. Rest 20 minutes.

> **Tip:** For steak, grilled vegetables, or roast chicken.
