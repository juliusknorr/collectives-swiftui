# Ragù alla bolognese

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 3 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 300 g minced beef, 200 g minced pork
- [ ] 100 g pancetta
- [ ] 1 onion, 1 carrot, 1 celery stick
- [ ] 150 ml white wine
- [ ] 200 ml milk
- [ ] 400 g tomato passata

## Steps

1. Finely dice the soffritto and cook slowly.
2. Brown the meat and pancetta.
3. Add wine and reduce, then milk and reduce.
4. Add passata, simmer very gently for 2.5 hours.

> **Tip:** Used by the lasagne in Pasta.
