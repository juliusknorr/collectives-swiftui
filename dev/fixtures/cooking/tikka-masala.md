# Chicken tikka masala

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 1 h + marinating |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 800 g chicken thighs
- [ ] 150 g yogurt
- [ ] 2 tbsp garam masala
- [ ] Ginger and garlic
- [ ] 400 g tomatoes
- [ ] 150 ml cream
- [ ] Fresh coriander

## Steps

1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, half the spices, ginger and garlic for 2 hours.
2. Grill or broil until charred.
3. Cook onion with the remaining spices, add tomatoes, simmer 15 minutes.
4. Blend, add cream and chicken, simmer 10 minutes.

> **Tip:** Serve with rice and naan from the Breads section.
