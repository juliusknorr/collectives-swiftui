## Sourdough starter feeding

1. Discard all but 50 g starter.
2. Add 50 g flour and 50 g water.
3. Use when doubled, 4 to 8 hours later.

## Knife skills

- **Julienne**: thin matchsticks
- **Brunoise**: tiny cubes from julienne
- **Chiffonade**: rolled and sliced leaves
