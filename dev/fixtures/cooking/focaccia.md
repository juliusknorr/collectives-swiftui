# Focaccia

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 1 tray | 4 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 500 g flour
- [ ] 400 ml water
- [ ] 7 g dried yeast
- [ ] 10 g salt
- [ ] 60 ml olive oil, plus more
- [ ] Rosemary, flaky salt

## Steps

1. Mix to a wet dough, rest 2 hours with a few folds.
2. Oil a tray generously, stretch the dough in.
3. Proof 1 hour, dimple with oily fingers.
4. Top with rosemary and salt, bake at 230 °C for 22 minutes.

> **Tip:** More oil than feels reasonable.
