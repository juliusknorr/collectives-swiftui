# Pho bò

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 3 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1.5 kg beef bones
- [ ] 1 onion and a thumb of ginger, charred
- [ ] 2 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cloves
- [ ] 2 tbsp fish sauce
- [ ] 400 g rice noodles
- [ ] 300 g sirloin, very thinly sliced
- [ ] Herbs, lime, chili, bean sprouts

## Steps

1. Blanch the bones 5 minutes, drain and rinse.
2. Simmer bones with charred onion, ginger and spices for at least 2.5 hours. Skim often.
3. Strain, season with fish sauce and a little sugar.
4. Pour boiling broth over noodles and raw beef.
5. Serve with herbs, lime and chili.

> **Tip:** Clear broth needs a gentle simmer, never a rolling boil.
