# Palak paneer

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 35 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 500 g spinach
- [ ] 250 g paneer
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 2 tomatoes
- [ ] Ginger, garlic, green chili
- [ ] 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp cumin
- [ ] 2 tbsp cream

## Steps

1. Blanch spinach, blend smooth.
2. Fry cubed paneer until golden.
3. Cook onion, aromatics, spices and tomatoes.
4. Add spinach and paneer, simmer 5 minutes, finish with cream.

> **Tip:** Blanching briefly keeps the color bright green.
