# Naan

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 2 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 300 g flour
- [ ] 1 tsp yeast
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar
- [ ] 150 g yogurt
- [ ] 80 ml warm water
- [ ] Butter and garlic

## Steps

1. Knead everything to a soft dough, rise 1 hour.
2. Divide into 6 and roll into ovals.
3. Cook in a very hot dry pan, flip when bubbles appear.
4. Brush with garlic butter.

> **Tip:** Pairs with the tikka masala from Mains.
