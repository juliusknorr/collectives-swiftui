# Roast chicken

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 1 h 30 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 chicken, about 1.6 kg
- [ ] 1 lemon
- [ ] Half a head of garlic
- [ ] Thyme
- [ ] Butter, salt, pepper

## Steps

1. Dry the chicken and salt it, ideally the night before.
2. Stuff with lemon, garlic and thyme, rub with butter.
3. Roast at 200 °C for about 1 hour 15 minutes, until juices run clear (75 °C inside).
4. Rest 15 minutes before carving.

> **Tip:** Make stock from the carcass for the Soups section.
