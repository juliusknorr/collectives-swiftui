# Lasagne

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 2 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] Ragù (see Sauces)
- [ ] Béchamel from 50 g butter, 50 g flour, 600 ml milk
- [ ] 250 g lasagne sheets
- [ ] 100 g parmesan

## Steps

1. Make the béchamel.
2. Layer ragù, sheets, béchamel and parmesan, four times.
3. Bake at 180 °C for 40 minutes.
4. Rest 15 minutes before cutting.

> **Tip:** Assemble the day before for cleaner slices.
