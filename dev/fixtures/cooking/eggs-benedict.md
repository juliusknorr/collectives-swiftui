# Eggs Benedict

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 25 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 English muffins
- [ ] 4 slices ham
- [ ] 4 very fresh eggs
- [ ] Splash of vinegar
- [ ] Hollandaise (see Sauces)

## Steps

1. Toast the muffins and warm the ham.
2. Poach the eggs in barely simmering water with vinegar for 3 minutes.
3. Stack muffin, ham, egg and spoon over hollandaise.

> **Tip:** Links to Hollandaise in the Sauces section.
