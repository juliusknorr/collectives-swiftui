# Lentil soup

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 40 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 250 g red lentils
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] 1 carrot
- [ ] 2 tsp cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp turmeric
- [ ] 1.2 l stock
- [ ] Lemon

## Steps

1. Soften onion and carrot.
2. Add spices, lentils and stock.
3. Simmer 25 minutes until creamy, blend partly.
4. Finish with lemon juice.

> **Tip:** Pantry-only recipe, ideal for offline testing on a train.
