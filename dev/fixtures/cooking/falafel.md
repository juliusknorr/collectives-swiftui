# Falafel

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 20 pieces | 30 min + soaking |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 250 g dried chickpeas, soaked overnight
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] Bunch of parsley and coriander
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander seed
- [ ] 1 tsp baking powder

## Steps

1. Pulse everything to a coarse crumb. Never use canned chickpeas.
2. Chill 30 minutes.
3. Shape into balls and fry at 175 °C until deep brown.

> **Tip:** Serve with tahini sauce and pickles.
