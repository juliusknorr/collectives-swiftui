# Ratatouille

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 1 h 15 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 eggplant
- [ ] 2 zucchini
- [ ] 2 peppers
- [ ] 4 tomatoes
- [ ] 1 onion
- [ ] Garlic, thyme, olive oil

## Steps

1. Cook each vegetable separately in olive oil until golden.
2. Combine with garlic, tomatoes and thyme.
3. Simmer gently for 30 minutes.

> **Tip:** Cooking separately keeps every vegetable distinct.
