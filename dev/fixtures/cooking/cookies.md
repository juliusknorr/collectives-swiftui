# Chocolate chip cookies

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 24 | 30 min + chilling |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 225 g butter, browned
- [ ] 200 g brown sugar, 100 g white sugar
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 300 g flour
- [ ] 1 tsp baking soda, 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 300 g dark chocolate, chopped

## Steps

1. Brown the butter and cool slightly.
2. Beat in sugars, then eggs.
3. Fold in dry ingredients and chocolate.
4. Chill dough 1 hour.
5. Bake at 180 °C for 10 to 12 minutes.

> **Tip:** Pull them out while the middle looks underdone. 🍪
