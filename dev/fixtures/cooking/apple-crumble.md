# Apple crumble

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 50 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 kg apples
- [ ] 1 tsp cinnamon
- [ ] 150 g flour
- [ ] 100 g cold butter
- [ ] 100 g brown sugar
- [ ] 50 g oats

## Steps

1. Slice apples, toss with cinnamon and a little sugar.
2. Rub butter into flour, sugar and oats.
3. Scatter over apples, bake at 190 °C for 35 minutes.

> **Tip:** Serve with custard or vanilla ice cream.
