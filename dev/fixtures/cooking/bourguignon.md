# Beef bourguignon

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 3 h 30 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1.2 kg beef chuck
- [ ] 150 g bacon lardons
- [ ] 750 ml red wine
- [ ] 2 carrots, 1 onion
- [ ] 250 g mushrooms
- [ ] 12 pearl onions
- [ ] 2 tbsp flour, tomato paste, bay, thyme

## Steps

1. Brown the beef in batches.
2. Fry lardons, carrot and onion, stir in flour and tomato paste.
3. Add wine, herbs and beef, braise at 160 °C for 3 hours.
4. Brown mushrooms and pearl onions separately and add at the end.

> **Tip:** Use a wine you would drink.
