# Cinnamon rolls

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 12 | 3 h |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 500 g flour
- [ ] 250 ml warm milk
- [ ] 7 g yeast
- [ ] 60 g sugar
- [ ] 1 egg
- [ ] 80 g soft butter
- [ ] Filling: 100 g butter, 150 g brown sugar, 2 tbsp cinnamon
- [ ] Cream cheese frosting

## Steps

1. Knead an enriched dough and rise 1 hour.
2. Roll out, spread filling, roll up and cut 12 slices.
3. Proof 45 minutes, bake at 180 °C for 25 minutes.
4. Frost while warm.
