# Overnight oats

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 5 min + overnight |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 50 g rolled oats
- [ ] 120 ml milk or oat drink
- [ ] 2 tbsp yogurt
- [ ] 1 tsp chia seeds
- [ ] 1 tsp honey
- [ ] Berries

## Steps

1. Stir everything except the berries in a jar.
2. Refrigerate overnight.
3. Top with berries in the morning.

> **Tip:** Keeps for 3 days in the fridge, so make a batch on Sunday.
