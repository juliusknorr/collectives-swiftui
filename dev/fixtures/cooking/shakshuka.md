# Shakshuka

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 2 | 30 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 onion, sliced
- [ ] 1 red pepper, sliced
- [ ] 2 garlic cloves
- [ ] 1 tsp cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 400 g chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 4 eggs
- [ ] Feta and parsley

## Steps

1. Soften onion and pepper in oil for 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic and spices, cook 1 minute.
3. Add tomatoes, simmer 10 minutes, season.
4. Make 4 wells, crack in the eggs, cover and cook until the whites set.
5. Top with feta and parsley.

> **Tip:** Serve straight from the pan with warm bread. 🍳
