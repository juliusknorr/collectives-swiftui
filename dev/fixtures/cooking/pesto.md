# Pesto genovese

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 10 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 large bunches basil
- [ ] 30 g pine nuts
- [ ] 1 garlic clove
- [ ] 40 g parmesan, 20 g pecorino
- [ ] 100 ml olive oil

## Steps

1. Pound garlic and pine nuts in a mortar.
2. Add basil and grind to a paste.
3. Stir in cheese and oil.

> **Tip:** A blender works. Pulse briefly to avoid heat.
