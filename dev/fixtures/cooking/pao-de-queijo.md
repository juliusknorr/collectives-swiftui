# Pão de queijo

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 20 pieces | 40 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 250 g tapioca starch
- [ ] 125 ml milk
- [ ] 60 ml oil
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 2 eggs
- [ ] 120 g grated parmesan

## Steps

1. Bring milk, oil and salt to a boil.
2. Pour over the starch and stir to a sticky dough. Cool 10 minutes.
3. Beat in the eggs one by one, then the cheese.
4. Roll into balls with oiled hands.
5. Bake at 200 °C for 20 to 25 minutes.

> **Tip:** Naturally gluten free. Freeze unbaked balls and bake straight from frozen.
