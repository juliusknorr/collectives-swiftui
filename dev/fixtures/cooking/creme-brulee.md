# Crème brûlée

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 6 | 1 h + chilling |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 500 ml cream
- [ ] 1 vanilla pod
- [ ] 6 egg yolks
- [ ] 80 g sugar, plus more for the top

## Steps

1. Infuse cream with vanilla.
2. Whisk yolks and sugar, pour over the warm cream.
3. Bake in ramekins in a water bath at 150 °C for 35 minutes.
4. Chill, then caramelize sugar on top with a torch.
