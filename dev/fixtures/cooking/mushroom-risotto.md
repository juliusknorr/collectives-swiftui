# Mushroom risotto

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 40 min |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 300 g carnaroli rice
- [ ] 400 g mixed mushrooms
- [ ] 20 g dried porcini
- [ ] 1 shallot
- [ ] 100 ml white wine
- [ ] 1.2 l hot stock
- [ ] Butter and parmesan

## Steps

1. Soak porcini, add the liquid to the stock.
2. Brown the mushrooms, set aside.
3. Toast rice with the shallot, deglaze with wine.
4. Add stock ladle by ladle, stirring, for 18 minutes.
5. Stir in mushrooms, butter and parmesan. Rest 2 minutes.

> **Tip:** Swap parmesan for a vegetarian hard cheese if needed.
