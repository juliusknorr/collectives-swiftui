# Tiramisu

| Serves | Time |
| --- | --- |
| 8 | 30 min + overnight |

## Ingredients

- [ ] 4 eggs, separated
- [ ] 100 g sugar
- [ ] 500 g mascarpone
- [ ] 300 ml strong espresso, cooled
- [ ] 2 tbsp marsala
- [ ] 250 g ladyfingers
- [ ] Cocoa powder

## Steps

1. Beat yolks and sugar until pale, fold in mascarpone.
2. Whip whites to stiff peaks and fold in.
3. Dip ladyfingers briefly in coffee and marsala.
4. Layer biscuits and cream twice.
5. Dust with cocoa and chill overnight.

> **Tip:** Dip fast. Soggy biscuits collapse.
